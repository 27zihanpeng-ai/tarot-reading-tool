## Major Arcana Tarot Card Prompts (Classic Mystical Style)

### 0. The Fool
**Prompt:**
A mystical tarot card illustration in classic Rider-Waite style. A young man in colorful clothing stands at a cliff edge, about to step into the unknown. He carries a small pack and a white rose, with a small white dog at his heels. The background shows a sunrise over vast landscapes. Soft, warm colors with gold accents. Ornate border frame.

### 1. The Magician
**Prompt:**

## How to Use These Prompts

1. Go to [Bing Image Creator](https://www.bing.com/images/create)
2. Copy each prompt one at a time
3. Generate 1 image per prompt
4. Download each image
5. Rename them: `00-fool.png`, `01-magician.png`, etc.
6. Place all images in a folder

**Total Time Required:** 20-30 minutes to generate all 22 images
